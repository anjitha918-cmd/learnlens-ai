Binary Search Tree

Data Structures · Beginner · Simple explanation

Understand the concept

A Binary Search Tree (BST) is a binary tree that keeps values in an ordered structure. For each node, values smaller than the node are placed in its left subtree, and larger values are placed in its right subtree.

Key points

  • Each node has at most two children: left and right.
  • All values in the left subtree are smaller than the node.
  • All values in the right subtree are larger than the node.
  • Inorder traversal visits the values in sorted order.

Example

Insert 50, 30 and 70. The root is 50; 30 becomes its left child and 70 becomes its right child.
